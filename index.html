Questions

Frequently asked questions

Voucher for Application?

Yes.GRA charges a fee of GHC 350 for forms.

Can I apply more than once?

No. Submit one application for one preferred position. Duplicate applications can lead to disqualification.

Where is training held?

Successful applicants train at the Customs Training School. Posting after training depends on operational needs.

Can I choose my duty station?

You may list preferences in the form. Final postings are determined by the Customs Division.

What happens after I submit?

This portal prepares a local acknowledgement preview with your Application ID. Keep it for your records and verify the next after 24 hours.

What if I miss the deadline?

Applications close on Sunday, 8 November 2026 at 10:00 AM GMT. The countdown on this page shows the remaining time.
